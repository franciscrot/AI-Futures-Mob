You are CEO of . How will you steer your organisation through the rapidly changing landscape of AI?

How to Play

  • Earn Progress Points and convert them into RAI Points.
  • You can play Action cards to prepare your organisation for future challenges.
  • You can also play Event cards, which trigger effects depending on the Actions already in play.
  • You will be competing alongside two other organisations.
  • Swipe left to see the next card in your hand. Swipe right to play the current card. Double-tap a card to ask to skip it.
  • You have one SKIP. Once it is used, certain Choice cards may recharge it.
